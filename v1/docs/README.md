study or its earlier development**. Words such as “final” refer to that stage,
not the current [Version 2 analysis](../../v2/README.md).

- [Workflow guide](PROJECT_WORKFLOWS.md): commands run from `v1/`.
- [Final-results summary](final_results_summary.md): original controlled comparison.

The original protocols and scalar provenance record retain their recorded
contents for checksum verification. Historical paths can refer to local
execution records or private reports that are not distributed here.
No manuscript source, Word report, or complete manuscript PDF is included.
